Revision note — August 2026 report

A record of source-data movement observed after this report was published. English first, then Japanese (site convention). 日本語版は こちら。

The published August 2026 report is a point-in-time snapshot frozen on 2026-08-13, and its content has not been changed. This note records changes MSRC made to the underlying data after that snapshot, using the MSRC CVRF and CISA KEV retrieved on 2026-11-12. The report was correct as a snapshot; the data has since moved.

What this note is

Each monthly report is a frozen snapshot of MSRC primary data taken on a specific day. MSRC revises past months after the fact — adding CVEs, changing severities, and updating exploitation status. The report itself already discloses this and preserves its snapshot values unchanged. This note is the companion record of the movement observed for the August 2026 data after publication, stated as facts. It does not correct or replace the report, and it does not interpret the movement.

CVE-2026-65660 (SharePoint) — KEV and the CVRF exploitation flag disagree

  • MSRC revision text (CVRF RevisionHistory, version 1.2, 2026-09-25): “As of 9/25/2026, Microsoft had reliable evidence of observed attacks against exploitation of this vulnerability.”
  • CISA KEV: added 2026-09-25.
  • CVRF exploitation flag: Exploited:No as of 2026-11-12 (Publicly Disclosed:No).
  • EPSS: 0.0985 (2026-11-10).

The revision text and KEV both refer to exploitation; the flag still says No. This note does not say which is right. At the freeze (2026-08-13) the CVE was not in KEV and the revision text did not yet exist.

Effect on statements in the report

Facts and figures stated in the report that no longer match the upstream data as of 2026-11-12 (each was correct at the freeze):

  • Appendix: “This month Kugelblitz appears once.” → 5 on 2026-11-12. The four added (CVE-2026-66323, 66324, 66798, 70341) are Edge advisories (excluded side, Important), first published 2026-08-28 (15 days after the freeze). They are advisories added after the freeze, not existing CVEs whose credit was rewritten. Counting only CVEs first published on or before the freeze day gives 1, matching the frozen value. None of the five is Critical.
  • Appendix: “Edge advisories themselves are down to under a tenth this month” → counting Edge advisories first published on or before each month’s freeze day gives August 39 and July 475 (8.2%), so it was correct at the freeze. On 2026-11-12 it is August 362 and July 936 (38.7%). Edge advisories were added to both months after their freezes.
  • Figure 3 (Kugelblitz): the August point (1) and its reading “After the July spike, back to one.” → 5 on 2026-11-12. The July point (39) is 58 on 2026-11-12 (see the 2026-11-12 addendum to the July note).
  • Appendix: the credit string “AIフィジカルインターフェイス二号機”, which “appears 13 times” → 16 on 2026-11-12. All 16 are CVEs first published on or before the freeze day; the credit was rewritten after the freeze. Which three cannot be identified without a freeze-time copy of the CVRF (five such CVEs have an acknowledgement update recorded after the freeze). The one “AI’s physical interface 001” credit is unchanged.
  • Zero-days: publicly disclosed went from 2 to 3 (CVE-2026-69414, Microsoft Defender elevation of privilege, Important; first published 2026-08-14, the day after the freeze). Exploitation confirmed is still the one CVE-2026-68820.

This note adds no interpretation to these changes, and nothing to the report’s “this decrease cannot be interpreted”.
